## Product constraints

- The entire game remains inside one fictional audio-restoration workstation.
- Every required clue must originate from the seven recordings, their channels, waveforms, session metadata, or restoration results.
- Music puzzles must not require perfect pitch, formal music theory, or prior audio-engineering knowledge.
- Every auditory clue needs a visual or textual accessibility equivalent.
- Puzzles should reveal different links in one continuous causal chain rather than repeatedly verifying the same answer.
- Do not use copyrighted songs or recognizable copyrighted melodies; all game music and audio must be original.
- The canonical truth must preserve both Chen Du's innocence of intentional killing and his responsibility for disabling the alarm.
- Chen Mo was present during the fire as a child; this revelation and the six-knock/one-note response must remain central to the emotional horror.
- A present-day second listener may alter only fictional in-game project state. Never imply access to the player's real microphone, camera, identity, files, or network.
- Horror should come from incomplete sounds, memory, live editorial interference, and moral complicity—not continuous noise, loud jump scares, or fake real-device intrusion.
- The player's ending choice may change what is published, but must not rewrite the established facts.
